Reading the field

Every arrow shows the force a positive test charge would feel. It points away from positive charges and toward negative ones; its opacity and length encode the local magnitude.

The simulator uses Coulomb’s law, E = kq/r², with positions in metres and charges in nanocoulombs. The potential view maps positive and negative electric potential around zero.
